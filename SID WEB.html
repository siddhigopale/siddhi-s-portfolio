About Me

I'm a multidisciplinary creative based in Pune, Maharashtra — equally at home in Adobe After Effects building motion sequences as I am writing a screenplay or crafting social media campaigns that stop the scroll.

My work spans the full creative spectrum: from concept and script to final rendered frame. I've designed marketing campaigns, edited election advertisement videos, built brand identities, and contributed original scenes to film projects. Every project I take on is an opportunity to tell a story better.

With hands-on experience at digital agencies, motion studios, and film production setups, I bring both craft precision and creative vision to every deliverable — whether it's a 30-second reel or a feature screenplay.
